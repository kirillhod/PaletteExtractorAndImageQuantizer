Help

Load an image, choose a color count, and compare the original with the reduced result. Export a palette strip for engines and pixel editors, or a reduced PNG for quick use.

M2 preview. Five extractors, fixed palettes (built-in and import), exact/LUT mapping, error diffusion, Bayer and blue-noise dithering, indexed PNG with tRNS, strip export, and reduced PNG export are working. ΔE00 QA and the full theory guide are M3.

How it works

  1. Build a 5-bit, alpha-weighted color histogram.
  2. Extract a palette or load a fixed one.
  3. Map pixels with exact nearest-color lookup or a 6-bit LUT.
  4. Optionally diffuse or threshold the quantization error.
  5. Pack indices, write PNG, decode it back, and compare.
